# YouTube API audito paraiška — juodraštis

Kam to reikia: kol projektas nepraėjęs YouTube API Services audito, **kiekvienas per API
įkeltas filmukas lieka privatus** ir jo nepaviešinsi net ranka. Auditas tą ribą nuima.

Paraiška teikiama per [YouTube API Services – Audit and Quota Extension Form](https://support.google.com/youtube/contact/yt_api_form).
Forma pildoma **angliškai**, todėl žemiau — paruošti atsakymai kopijavimui.

⛔ Prieš siunčiant patikrink tris dalykus: projekto numerį, kanalo URL ir ar aprašyti
scope'ai sutampa su tais, kurių iš tikrųjų prašo įrankis (`youtube.upload`,
`youtube.force-ssl`).

### 1. What does your API Client do? (paskirtis)

> Cleverphant is a Lithuanian company that builds and maintains websites for schools
> (currently 250+ schools). We produce short educational videos that explain how school
> staff — administrators, secretaries, teachers — use their school website and its content
> management system.
>
> The videos are generated by our own internal pipeline: illustrations are rendered from
> HTML templates, narration is synthesised, and the final file is assembled with ffmpeg.
> Until now the last step — uploading the finished file to our own YouTube channel — was
> done by hand.
>
> This API client automates only that last step. For each finished video it uploads the
> file, sets the localized title and description, attaches subtitle tracks, sets a
> thumbnail, and adds the video to a playlist. It uploads only to YouTube channels owned
> by our company, and only videos we produced ourselves.

### 2. Who uses it? (naudotojai)

> One person — the company owner, who is also the owner of the YouTube channels. The tool
> runs locally on that person's computer from the command line. It is not installed by
> anyone else, not distributed, not offered as a service, and has no end users other than
> the channel owner. There is no user-facing interface and no sign-up.

### 3. What data does it process? (duomenys)

> The client processes only content we create ourselves:
>
> - the video file produced by our own pipeline;
> - title, description and tags written by us;
> - subtitle files generated from our own narration script;
> - an optional thumbnail image we designed.
>
> It does not collect, store, or process data about YouTube users. It does not read viewer
> data, comments, analytics, or any other person's content. It does not access channels
> other than our own. OAuth credentials are stored only on the local machine, in a
> directory excluded from version control, and are never transmitted anywhere except to
> Google's own API endpoints.

### 4. Which API methods do you call? (kvietimai)

> - `videos.insert` — upload the finished video (always with `privacyStatus: private`;
>   publishing is a separate, manual human decision);
> - `videos.update` — set localized titles and descriptions;
> - `thumbnails.set` — set the thumbnail;
> - `captions.insert` — attach subtitle tracks;
> - `playlists.list`, `playlists.insert`, `playlistItems.insert` — place the video in the
>   right playlist;
> - `videos.list` — verify after upload that the video exists and has the expected metadata;
> - `channels.list` — confirm which channel the tool is authenticated against.

### 5. How do you comply with the Developer Policies? (atitiktis)

> - The client uploads only original content produced by our company and only to channels
>   we own.
> - Every upload is set to `private`. A human reviews the video and publishes it manually,
>   or sets a scheduled publish time explicitly.
> - The client never uploads content marked as made for children; the audience is adult
>   school staff, and this is enforced in validation.
> - The client performs no automated actions on other people's videos or channels: no
>   comments, no likes, no subscriptions, no viewing.
> - The client keeps a local log and never uploads the same video twice.
> - Quota is respected: on `quotaExceeded` the client stops and resumes the next day rather
>   than retrying in a loop.

### 6. Why do you need a quota increase? (jei prašoma kvotos)

*(Pildyti tik tada, kai 10 000 vienetų per parą tikrai neužtenka.)*

> We publish a small number of videos — a handful per month per market. The default quota
> is sufficient for normal operation; we are applying for the audit so that our own
> uploaded videos are not permanently locked to private, not because we need more quota.

## Po audito

Kai auditas praeitas — `config/settings.json` nekeičiamas, `privacy` **lieka `private`**.
Tai sąmoningas sprendimas: viešinimas yra žmogaus žingsnis po peržiūros, o ne įkėlimo
šalutinis poveikis.
