## Spine

Detail → problem → what it is → one network, three directions (bug, idea, announcement) → equally good → Trojan elephant → scale → proof.

**The core (Eimantas 2026-10-12): show how easily everything scales.** It is not a separate scene — it is the
film's recurring motion. One visual carries it: **the wave from one to all.** Whatever happens in one place
spreads across the whole network in the same calm wave:

| Scene | What spreads in the wave |
|---|---|
| 1 | a mistake — one capitals heading becomes a thousand (the problem scales too) |
| 4 | a fix — one school notices, all are fixed |
| 5 | an idea — one pilot becomes everyone's standard |
| 6 | an announcement — published once, on every site |
| 8 | each step of the ladder — it lands on all sites at once |
| 9 | the network itself — one school → municipality → region → country |
| 10 | **the hook returns:** the same thousand tiles from scene 1, all fixed in a single wave |

⛔ „Easy“ is shown by **effortlessness, not speed**. No „in minutes“, no counters racing: the wave is calm
and the same every time. Real change in the public sector does not happen in five minutes (Eimantas).
The ease is that nothing has to be done school by school.

**Visual:** dalis A kaip v5 (dramblys eina pro svetainę; didžiosios raidės, išlygintas tekstas, tekstas paveikslėlyje, slapukų sutikimas). **Voice:** Change starts with details. Being small is no excuse to look away. Stop writing in capitals, stop stretching text, stop hiding information in images, get cookie consent right. That's our Trojan elephant, in the good sense. Once it works everywhere, we go further: a document bank, whistleblower protection, bookings, and next, AI integrations. Because AI can't work in chaos.
