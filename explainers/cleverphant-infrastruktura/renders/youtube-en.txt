Cleverphant explained: shared digital infrastructure for schools

Cleverphant is shared digital infrastructure for schools. What people see is the website. Underneath is everything that keeps it right: one design system, compliance, training and support, shared by every school. This explainer shows how a network of school websites works and why it beats hundreds of separate ones.

What the video covers:
• Why a school website is the only channel a school fully controls
• What every school faces alone: accessibility, data protection, security, changing rules
• Stop replacing the website every few years. Start growing one.
• One design system: improve one part, and every page of every school improves
• Not an agency, not a website builder: we move the old website, the school checks, it goes live
• Fixed once, fixed for all. A pilot in one school becomes the standard for everyone.
• The Trojan elephant: continuous small updates instead of big reforms
• Fifteen years, almost 300 institutions, clients in 85% of Lithuania's municipalities
