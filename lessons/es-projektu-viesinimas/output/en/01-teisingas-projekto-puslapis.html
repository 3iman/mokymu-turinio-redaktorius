Project “Opportunity School”
Co-funded by the European Union
School name participates as a partner in the project “Opportunity School” No. 10-072-P-0001, funded by EU structural funds.
The goal of this project is to strengthen the quality of education that meets the age and needs of students.
Project implementation – from April 2024 to July 2027.
Project executor – National Education Agency.
Total project budget – EUR 19,187,021.64.
